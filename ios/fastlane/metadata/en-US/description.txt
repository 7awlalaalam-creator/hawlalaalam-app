Around The World: Quran is your daily companion with the Book of Allah — a non-profit app, completely free and ad-free. The app interface is in Arabic.

The Mushaf
• The complete Quran (Hafs 'an 'Asim, Uthmani script), fully offline.
• Tafsir al-Muyassar for every verse, verse search, bookmarks and notes.
• Remembers where you stopped so you can continue reading.

Smart khatma
• Choose how long you want to take and which days you read; your daily portion is planned for you.
• Tracks your progress and re-plans if you fall behind or get ahead.
• A gentle daily reminder at the time you choose.

Prayer
• Prayer times for your city, with several calculation methods including Umm al-Qura.
• Adhan notification for every prayer, plus sunrise.
• Qibla compass.

Adhkar & tasbeeh
• Morning, evening and after-prayer adhkar.
• Digital tasbeeh with 33 / 99 / 100 targets.
• Reminders for Surah Al-Kahf on Friday and Sunnah fasting days.

Recitations
• Listen to renowned reciters and download surahs for offline listening, with lock-screen playback.

And more
• Verse of the day, sharing verses as beautiful cards, home-screen widgets (prayer times, verse of the day, khatma, Ramadan), dark mode and adjustable text size.

Private by design
• No accounts, no sign-up — your data stays on your device.
