Thank you for reviewing Quran Around The World (القرآن حول العالم), a free, non-profit, ad-free Quran app. The interface is in Arabic. No account or sign-in is needed.

Native features to try:
- Mushaf tab: the full Quran works completely offline (bundled data), with tafsir, search and bookmarks.
- Today tab: next prayer, smart khatma progress ring, verse of the day.
- Tools > Khatma (ختمتي): create a reading plan; daily reminders are scheduled locally.
- Settings: enable adhan notifications (local notifications per prayer time), dark mode, text size.
- Tools > Qibla (القبلة): compass (needs a real device), Tasbeeh (المسبحة), Mood verses (آية لحالك).
- Listen tab: download a surah for offline playback with lock-screen controls.
- Home screen widgets: prayer times, verse of the day, khatma progress, Ramadan.

Location is used only to calculate prayer times and qibla direction and never leaves the device.
